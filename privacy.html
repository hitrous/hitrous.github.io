Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes the "Slow Spanish Daily Uploader" application, which is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Slow Spanish Daily.

What the app does

The app uploads lesson videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the Slow Spanish Daily YouTube channel and adds them to the channel's playlists. It uses YouTube API Services for this.

What data the app uses

The app accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, through a Google sign-in that the owner authorized. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users. Access tokens are stored privately and used only to upload the channel's own videos.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information on the Slow Spanish Daily channel page.
