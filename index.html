Easy Spanish for English speakers. Listen, repeat, speak.

Slow Spanish Daily is a free YouTube channel that teaches Spanish by ear with the step-by-step translation method. You hear a phrase in English, try to say it in Spanish, then hear the Spanish slowly and at normal speed and repeat it out loud.

  • Daily Shorts with one useful phrase
